Common Water Heater Problems We Fix

Water heater trouble can appear without warning. If you're seeing any of these problems, contact our team right away for prompt help.

Hot Water Gone Cold

A tripped high-limit switch, a failed gas valve, or a dead igniter can all look like the same symptom. Real diagnosis beats parts-swapping every time.

Same-day repair details

Water Around the Heater

A dripping temperature and pressure relief valve is a warning sign, not a nuisance. It usually means excess pressure or heat, often from a missing expansion tank.

Popping Noises, Smelly Water

Rusty hot water can mean the anode rod is spent and the tank is starting to corrode. Catching it early can add years to the heater.
